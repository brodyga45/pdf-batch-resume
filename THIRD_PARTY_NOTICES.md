# Third-party components

Dependencies are installed separately. This source archive includes no vendored dependency code.

- **Pillow 12.3.0** — MIT-CMU, with additional bundled library notices in its distribution. Full license: https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow/blob/12.3.0/LICENSE . Performs image decoding, resizing and encoding.
- **ReportLab 4.4.9** — BSD-style license, used only by optional synthetic tests. Project: https://pypi.org/project/reportlab/4.4.9/ ; installed full license copied to `licenses/reportlab-LICENSE.txt` . Not required for ordinary use.
